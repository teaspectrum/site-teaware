Contribution rules

  • Each file is one language version of an object; Chinese and English share id, type and slug.
  • Migrated content defaults to needs-review. Do not mark automated or unchecked content as reviewed.
  • When evidence is missing, keep a blank or “to verify” note. Never invent coordinates, opening status, quotations or authorship.
  • Keep teahouse history separate from current operating information; historic route distances and difficulty are not modern hiking guidance.
  • Images, modern editions and translations require separate rights checks.
  • Run the catalogue generator and validators before submitting; commit README/CATALOG with the article.
