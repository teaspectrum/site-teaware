Cast iron is a teaware material. Discuss observable behaviour: heat transfer, touch, odour, durability, cleaning and safety, not a single “breathability” claim.

Key points

  • Cast iron is a teaware material. Discuss observable behaviour: heat transfer, touch, odour, durability, cleaning and safety, not a single “breathability” claim.
  • Separate concept, variables and setting before copying a parameter.
  • With mould, sourness, clumping or unknown source, prioritise food safety.

Context

This page places Cast iron inside the framework of 茶器材质: what problem it solves, which variables matter, and how to judge the result.

Use first

An object must complete an action: taking leaf, pouring, decanting, serving, holding waste or storing. If the action is awkward, expensive material will still end up unused.

Form and material

Capacity and proportion matter more than decoration. A wide mouth loses heat, a short spout drips, an over-fine strainer clogs; all change the rhythm of a brew.

Care

Wash, rinse and dry before first use. Avoid sudden temperature change, especially in clay, porcelain and glass. Clean with warm water and a soft cloth; tea stain is not “patina”.

Common misreadings

  • “Material decides everything”: Form, capacity and handling matter just as much.
  • “More expensive is more comfortable”: Price may reflect craft, maker or rarity, not fit.

Practical advice

Choose by the most frequent action and number of people; handle before buying; start simple and easy to clean; build a drying habit.

Rust and iron-pot care

Rust inside an iron pot comes from standing water and not drying after boiling. Empty it, dry with residual heat, and do not scrub the interior with detergent; wipe the outside with a soft cloth. It is for heating water, not long tea storage or boiling tea, which adds an iron taste. Follow the maker’s advice for coated interiors and never heat dry.

Source boundary

This page can establish how form, material, capacity and care affect use. It cannot prove that one material improves every tea or that price equals comfort.
